Expected wins and luck

Expected Wins

Each week, a team gets credit for every other team it outscored (ties count 0.5), divided by the number of opponents (N − 1). Those weekly values are summed. Luck is actual wins minus expected wins.

Luck

Actual wins minus expected wins. Positive means more wins than the scores deserved.
